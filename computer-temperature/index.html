I just realized that my ThinkPad T460p gets really loud. I suspect that there is quite some dust in the machine, so that the fan gets louder. But I'm not sure how to check that and I hesitate to open the machine. So let's see which information I can get from the software.

My System

  • Hardware: Lenovo ThinkPad T460p
  • Software: Ubuntu 18.04
  • Running: Chrome (no videos playing; no Flash), Sublime Text - nothing fancy

Make it Silent

I installed thermald and soon after that it got more silent. That could be a coincidence, though.

Intel vs Nvidia

Changing from Nvidia to Intel dropped the CPU temperature from roughly 50°C to roughly 40°C and the SSD to 38°C.
