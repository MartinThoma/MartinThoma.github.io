It’s New Year’s Eve and — as always — I try to finish some things and have some plans for next year.

## Review of 2020

The year 2020 was crazy. It was mainly dominated by the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), but also by a lot of crazy news by Trump / from the US. There were also [bushfires in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Australian_bushfire_season) and [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_California_wildfires), the [Atlantic Hurricane season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Atlantic_hurricane_season), the [Pacific typhoon season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Pacific_typhoon_season), a [locust infestation in East Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_locust_infestation), severe [floods in Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Jakarta_floods). Oh, and there was [Brexit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit), the [Killing of George Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_George_Floyd), and the [Wirecard Scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirecard_scandal). It was an exhausting year.

There were also positive things in 2020:

* **Climate Change**: Due to COVID-19 we had lockdowns. Due to the lockdowns, we got a [7% reduction in CO2 emissions](https://www.dw.com/en/global-carbon-emissions-down-by-record-7-in-2020/a-55900887). Some of those effects might be permanent: More people taking bikes, more people working from home.
* **Air Quality**: Also due to the lockdowns, the [air quality in cities improved](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11869-020-00963-y).
* **Renewable Energy**: [New renewable energy capacity hit a record](https://www.reuters.com/article/uk-iea-renewables/record-new-renewable-energy-capacity-this-year-and-next-iea-idUKKBN27Q0MR).
* **Fighting Diseases**: [Africa is free of wild polio](https://www.who.int/news/item/25-08-2020-global-polio-eradication-initiative-applauds-who-african-region-for-wild-polio-free-certification). Also, I think the many vaccines we developed to fight SARS-CoV-2 are quite a success story.
* **AI development**: [GPT-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-3) and [AlphaFold 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaFold#AlphaFold_2,_2020) directly pop to my mind.
* **Movies, Series, and YouTube**: Most major productions were not released in 2020. I remember that I’ve watched a lot of [Jane the Virgin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_the_Virgin) with my girlfriend, but I’m not super excited about it. The [Jordan Peterson interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aMcjxSThD54) was pretty interesting. The guest is calm, makes his points very well, speaks up when he is (purposely) misunderstood. The interviewer tackles hard the potential flaws of the argument. She does not try to make this nice but instead tries to get interesting answers. I’ve discovered and like the [TierZoo channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCHsRtomD4twRf5WVHHk-cMw).
* **Books**: I liked Martha Wells’s “The Murderbot Diaries” and Trudi Canavan’s “Millennium’s Rule”.
* **Breakthroughs**: [MIP\*=RE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04383), [Conway Knot Problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_knot)

Personally, I can see some stats going up:

* I have 85,272 points (+16,629) on **Stack Overflow** and reached 37.5 million people (+7.6 million) with my posts. ([source](https://stackoverflow.com/users/562769/martin-thoma))
* I wrote 73 articles for [my **blog**](http://martin-thoma.com/). Of those, 55 were published on Medium. I’ve got some experience with the various publications. It’s quite fun!
* My engagement for **Wikimedia Commons** stopped. I wasn't even aware of it, before I compared [my current edit/upload count](https://ptools.toolforge.org/uploadsum.php?user=MartinThoma) with [stats from last year](../new-year-2020/).
* I did A LOT for **open source projects** this year. It was mostly a bit behind the scenes, supporting smaller projects and people with learning how to write good code and how to make packages for Python. I’ve created [flake8-simplify](https://github.com/MartinThoma/flake8-simplify) and applied it to [openshot-qt](https://github.com/MartinThoma/openshot-qt), [openpyxl-stubs](https://github.com/MartinThoma/openpyxl-stubs), [munch-stubs](https://github.com/MartinThoma/munch-stubs), [Flask-HTTPAuth-stubs](https://pypi.org/project/Flask-HTTPAuth-stubs/), [puremagic](https://github.com/MartinThoma/puremagic), [fbro](https://github.com/MartinThoma/fbro). I supported [biopython](https://github.com/biopython/biopython), and ported the related package [propy3](https://pypi.org/project/propy3) to Python 3.

### Technology

1. **Battery Technology**: I expect to see more improvements. One way to prove this would be a Smartphone (&lt; 7" display size) with 5500mAh or more. The Galaxy S20 Ultra has 5000mAh and the iPhone 11 Pro Max has 3969mAh. Another way to see it would be a car with at least 600 km range. The [Tesla Model 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Model_3) has a range of 568km in the most extreme configuration.
1. **Self-driving Cars**: Again, I expect that no self-driving car will be on German streets (except for test drives with a driver who is responsible in the seat)
1. **Neural Network Size**: Neural Networks already became crazy big. T-NLG has 17 billion parameters ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/turing-nlg-a-17-billion-parameter-language-model-by-microsoft/)). GPT-3 has 175 billion parameters. I expect to see a 200 billion parameter model in 2021.
