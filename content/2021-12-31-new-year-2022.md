It’s New Year’s Eve and — as always — I finish some things and have some plans for next year.

## Review of 2021

Oh man, what a year. 2021 was — again! — dominated by the [**COVID-19 pandemic**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic). This came as a surprise to me as we’ve learned that masks are really useful in [April 2020 in Germany](https://www.spiegel.de/politik/deutschland/coronavirus-bund-und-laender-empfehlen-dringend-schutzmasken-in-geschaeften-a-d74e7df2-c79b-4699-9f99-e3615598137f) and the Pfizer–BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine was officially allowed in Germany end of 2020. Meaning we had a full year in which we got only [71.1% fully vaccinated in Germany](https://impfdashboard.de/en/). I see a lot of egocentric stupid movements around the world: [QAnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QAnon) trying to dismantle democracy in the US, the so called “Querdenker” in Germany who don’t believe Corona exists/that the vaccination contains microchips and is made by Lizard people, [people protesting](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-59363256) against wearing masks in France and the Netherlands. They also get legitimization, e.g. QAnon by many Republicans and big “news” outlets like Fox News or the “Querdenker” movement in Germany by the right-wing party [AfD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_for_Germany).

We still face several shortages, e.g. a [microchip shortage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_global_chip_shortage). Hahaha, I’ve just noticed the irony of writing about crazy people who think they are used within the vaccination 😁 The [**global supply chain issues**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_global_supply_chain_crisis) affect a lot of other stuff. I’ve heard of Aluminum, lumber for houses and paper for books, gas for heating. [Energy in general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_global_energy_crisis). I wanted to buy a painted mini-fridge for a colleague and was told that they are out of stock for the next 24 months. Crazy. Two reasons for those shortages are the [Suez Canal obstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Suez_Canal_obstruction) by the grounded ship “Ever Given” as well as the increased demand after the lockdowns. Corona again.

We also see [**inflation rise**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_inflation_surge). We have seen several **severe security issues** such as [Log4Shell](../the-log4j-javageddon/), [FORCEDENTRY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FORCEDENTRY), [PrintNightmare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PrintNightmare), [Silver Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Sparrow_%28malware%29), the [Zhenhua Data Leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhenhua_Data_leak), and the [Epik data breach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Epik_data_breach). There were several **attacks on or issues with infrastructure** such as [Transnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnet_ransomware_attack), the [Facebook outage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Facebook_outage), [managed service providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaseya_VSA_ransomware_attack), [meat processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JBS_S.A._cyberattack), [New Zealand Healthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waikato_District_Health_Board_ransomware_attack), [Healthcare in Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Service_Executive_ransomware_attack), the [Colonial pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Pipeline_ransomware_attack), [Pulse Connect VPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanti_Pulse_Connect_Secure_data_breach), [Microsoft Exchange Server data breach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Microsoft_Exchange_Server_data_breach).

The [**floods in Europe**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_European_floods) hit a lot of people hard. In Germany, we had cities where the first level of all houses was completely underwater. There is a clear link to climate change.

While negative news is more dominantly on my mind, there was quite a bit of positive news as well:

* **Labor improvements**: We see the [Great Resignation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Resignation) in which many people quit shitty jobs. A workers union was formed for Google ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_Workers_Union)).
* **GameStop**: Everybody likes David-vs-Goliath stories. Besides that, I’m uncertain of the effects of the [GameStop short squeeze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameStop_short_squeeze).
* **Breakthroughs in Science**: Something around [time crystals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_crystal) which I didn’t understand.
* **Breakthroughs in Technology / Engineering**:
    * The [Ingenuity Mars Helicopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingenuity_%28helicopter%29) is flying on Mars.
    * The [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope) was successfully launched to space this year. It will allow us to understand the origins of the universe, far-away planets, and galaxies way better.
    * Google successfully approached the chip floorplanning problem with reinforcement learning ([source](https://www.theverge.com/2021/6/10/22527476/google-machine-learning-chip-design-tpu-floorplanning))
    * The [Apple M1 Pro and M1 Max chips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_M1_Pro_and_M1_Max) were released. It might not be as ground-breaking as the rest of the news here, but it might indicate a shift in processor design.
* **Whistleblowing**: The [Pandora Papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandora_Papers) showed a lot of corruption and tax fraud/tax evasion. It’s positive as this helps to change the situation.

I was looking for a job where I can work from home as long/often as I want. [Cashlink](https://cashlink.de/) is remote-first, meaning I can come to the office if and when I like. This is amazing 🥳

* I have 101,005 points (+15,733) on **Stack Overflow** and reached 45.1 million people (+7.6 million) with my posts ([source](https://stackoverflow.com/users/562769/martin-thoma)). This is mostly due to the fact that I already have a lot of points. I’ve started to support people on [codereview.stackexchange](https://codereview.stackexchange.com/users/4433/martin-thoma?tab=profile) (1,145 points by 12 answers so far).
* My engagement for **Wikimedia Commons** stopped. I wasn’t even aware of it, before I compared [my current edit/upload count](https://ptools.toolforge.org/uploadsum.php?user=MartinThoma) with [stats from last year](../new-year-2020/). I didn’t pick it up again 😞
* The same goes for my contributions to **open-source projects** this year. I continued my work on [flake8-simplify](https://github.com/MartinThoma/flake8-simplify) and applied it to [openshot-qt](https://github.com/MartinThoma/openshot-qt). 1. ❌ [**German Federal Election**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_German_federal_election): I expect the conservatives (CDU/CSU) to reach at least 35% (it was 32.9% in 2017) — they only got 24.1% ❌. The social-democratic party SPD will not have more than 18% (20.5% in 2017) — they got 25.7% ❌. The greens will get more than 15% (8.9% in 2017) — they got 14.8% ❌.
1. ❌ **The Stock Market**: I expect the S&P 500 to end between 4030 and 4180 points (+8% to +12% in 2021): The S&P 500 is at 4778 in December 2021. Crazy. That’s +29.1%.
1. ❌ **Unemployment**: I thought unemployment would be a severe issue due to the pandemic, but it barely changed in Germany. It even went down quite a bit in the US. France, Italy, and Spain also improved. Why didn’t the pandemic have an effect on them?

### Technology

1. ❌ **Battery Technology**: The [best Android phone](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Samsung-Galaxy-S21-Ultra-Smartphone-Review-Improved-in-many-ways-but-not-all.517524.0.html) still has only 5000 mAh and the iPhone 13 Pro Max has 4352 mAh. I’m still waiting for getting those technologies industrial-scale production-ready.

## Predictions for 2022

### The Bigger Picture: Politics, Nature and the World

1. **Climate crisis**: I expect that politics in Germany and the US will focus on the economy after the pandemic and ignore the climate crisis.
1. We will see **more extreme weather** events like droughts, strong rain, extreme wind, bush fires, heat waves…
1. **No more Corona**: The COVID-19 pandemic will no longer be a relevant point of discussion in Germany after April 2022 🤞 I don’t think it will go away, but with vaccines and the highly infectious omicron variant I’m convinced it will become endemic. People will either be vaccinated or be recovered from an infection.

### Technology

4. **Quantum Computing**: [IBM Eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Eagle) has 127-qubits. I don’t expect big improvements here, but I do expect big players like Google, Intel, or China to get to a similar range.

5. **Battery Tech and Fusion**: I expect exciting news, but also no industry-scale solutions. They are just hard to achieve.

6. **Machine Learning**: I expect generative models to be improved and used more widely, e.g. in games in which content is dynamically created or more free interactions with the world are possible.

7. **Crypto / DLT**: I expect the market capitalization of crypto projects to grow a lot. It’s currently at 1.99 Trillion EUR ([source](https://coinmarketcap.com/de/)) whereas the market capitalization of Gold is 11.6 Trillion EUR ([source](https://companiesmarketcap.com/gold/marketcap/)). I expect the crypto / DLT market to grow over 2.2 Trillion EUR in 2022. I don’t expect the Bitcoin ATH to grow from 60.8k EUR in 2022.
