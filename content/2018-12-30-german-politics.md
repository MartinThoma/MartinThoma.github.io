<div class="info">This is an article I had for quite a while as a draft. As part of my yearly cleanup, I've published it without finishing it. It might not be finished or have other problems.</div>

This article is mostly a wish list. Things I would like to change in Germany,
where I have an idea in which direction the change should go. For some, I don't
know the details (and details are super important), though.

Each topic shows how important I think it is (❗ to ❗❗❗) and how simple a change
would be (✔ simple to ✔✔✔ very simple, ✘ hard to ✘✘✘ extremely hard).

<details id="mandatory-public-health-insurance">
<summary><span>Healthcare: Single mandatory public health insurance</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: high" title="Importance: high">❗❗❗</span> <span class="amount s-missing" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very hard" title="Simplicity: very hard">✘✘</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>In Germany, people can opt out of public health insurance and use a private
insurance instead. This is a problem as they usually opt out because they would
otherwise pay more. And it is a problem as those people might be in need of it
later.</p>
<p><strong>The solution</strong>: Don't allow opting out of public health insurance. I'm also not
too sure if the most severe cases should not be covered by a single insurance
for everybody which covers the basics. Other insurances can still exist, but
only as an additional service. For example, I could imagine parts in dental
care or glasses to be covered separately, while I expect anything
life-threatening or painful to go to the public health insurance.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="open-online-education">
<summary><span>Education: Open online education</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: medium" title="Importance: medium">❗❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: simple" title="Simplicity: simple">✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>I would like to have a big, well-designed, open platform with all necessary
educational information. For free. For everybody. Forever.</p>
<p>The idea is that Germany pays for two main pillars: (1) Initial development
(2) initial content.</p>
<p>Then it is handed over to a non-profit organization like Wikimedia for
operation.</p>
<p>The initial content could be provided by teachers. Pay them for approximately
two years to create the content of one year of their subject. That is a lot of
time for the teacher, but actually very little money for the content. Quality
assurance is important, though. So maybe there needs to be a competition where
teachers give samples of what they would hand in over that year.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="chained-short-term-contracts">
<summary><span>Employment: Chained short-term contracts</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: medium" title="Importance: medium">❗❗</span> <span class="amount s-missing" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: hard" title="Simplicity: hard">✘</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>Some companies and official institutions make only limited contracts (and often
even only short ones). This is a problem for the employees as it is a loophole
for some social security laws. It is also a problem if those people want to get
a loan. Not to mention the additional paperwork it creates.</p>
<p>See <a href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kettenvertrag">Kettenvertrag</a> for more
information.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="cookie-law">
<summary><span>Internet: Cookie Law</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: low" title="Importance: low">❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very simple" title="Simplicity: very simple">✔✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>The Problem: Virtually every website nowadays screams at you that it uses cookies.
This is not nice from a
<a href="https://ux.stackexchange.com/questions/40687/we-use-cookies-notice-on-websites">UX perspective</a>,
it is useless from a privacy perspective and it might <a href="http://www.dkriesel.com/blog/2018/0522_dsgvo_for_the_win">actively harm small independent websites</a>.</p>
<p><strong>The solution</strong>: Revert it. Completely. I don't see any use whatsoever for the user. On the contrary, it just makes websites worse.</p>
<p>A solution for people is to use add-ons like <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/i-dont-care-about-cookies/fihnjjcciajhdojfnbdddfaoknhalnja">I don't care about cookies</a>.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="declare-place-of-residency">
<summary><span>E-Governance: Declare Place of Residency</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: low" title="Importance: low">❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: simple" title="Simplicity: simple">✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>You have to declare where you live. When you move within Germany, you have to
go to the town hall and say where you moved from / to. You even need a written
confirmation by your landlord.</p>
<p>This is a problem because it is time-consuming. Yes, I can see that you want
authorities to know who lives in the city / where people moved, for example for
law enforcement. But it should be simpler.</p>
<p><strong>The solution</strong>: Make it digital.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="mandatory-software-updates">
<summary><span>Consumer protection: Mandatory software updates</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: low" title="Importance: low">❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very simple" title="Simplicity: very simple">✔✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>Devices which cost more than 20 EUR should have mandatory software updates.
Like CCC proposes so often, I would at the very least expect a labeling
requirement:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>The software of this router / smartphone / smartwatch will expire in 2019-01.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Better would be at least 2 years of guaranteed updates - if a critical bug is
discovered, an update has to be created within 5 working days. Something like
that.</p>
<p>A first step would be to have a clear way to update devices. A next step could
be to force companies to publish their source code in case of bankruptcy.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details id="pay-of-politicians">
<summary><span>Pay of politicians</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: low" title="Importance: low">❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very simple" title="Simplicity: very simple">✔✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>The pay (German: "Diäten") of Germany's
members of parliament is set by the parliament. This is a constant reason for
rage for people who earn very little. "While their situation gets worse, the
politicians put the money in their pockets" or similar thoughts.</p>
<p>I think that members of parliament (MP) should be paid really well. It is a hard
job and an important one. But it should also be connected to performance to
align the desires of the general population with the personal goals of MPs. And
it should be updated automatically so that MPs can focus on politics.</p>
<p>A simple way to achieve this would be to give MPs 4× the median income
of working people in Germany. This would be roughly equal to the amount they get
right now. If they want to earn more, they have to make sure that 50% of the
population earns more.</p>
<p>According to <a href="https://www.focus.de/finanzen/karriere/einkommensverteilung-nach-perzentilen-so-viele-menschen-verdienen-in-deutschland-weniger-als-sie_id_10552510.html">Focus 2019</a>,
the gross income of the lowest-earning 10% was 1201 EUR. So if we would like to
connect the ambitions of MPs more to the poorer parts of the population, we
could give them 8.3× the income of the 10th percentile.</p>
<p>See also:</p>
<ul>
<li>🇩🇪 <a href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einkommensverteilung_in_Deutschland">Einkommensverteilung in Deutschland</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>

<details id="fighting-corruption">
<summary><span>Fighting Corruption</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: medium" title="Importance: medium">❗❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very simple" title="Simplicity: very simple">✔✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>There are some simple measures to fight corruption:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Income Transparency</strong>: Every member of parliament should have to make their

  do so or purposely wrong statements should be a crime which is punished by
  having to pay back the money plus a fine, losing pensions, and prison time.</li>
<li><strong>Lobby Register</strong>: See details <a href="https://www.abgeordnetenwatch.de/lobbyregister-jetzt">here</a>.
  This is blocked by CDU / CSU.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<details id="consumer-protection-homeopathy">
<summary><span>Consumer Protection: Homeopathy</span> <span class="amount s-wip" role="img" aria-label="Importance: medium" title="Importance: medium">❗❗</span> <span class="amount s-ok" role="img" aria-label="Simplicity: very simple" title="Simplicity: very simple">✔✔✔</span></summary>
<div class="panel">
<p>Homeopathy is perceived as medicine. However, it does not have to follow the rules
of medicine. Normal medicine has to prove that it provides value in a study.
Homeopathy only has to prove that it doesn't do harm. It's not medicine. Typically,
it's 100% sugar. It's a scam.</p>
<p>The main problem is not the scam. The main problem is when people stop using
effective treatment and use the scam instead (<a href="https://twitter.com/drluebbers/status/1388037368033255425">example</a>).</p>
<p>It shouldn't be forbidden, but it should be transparent. It should contain a
clear list of ingredients with the amount of it and the common names (e.g. "sugar" instead of "glucose").
If possible, it should not be sold in pharmacies so that people don't even start
thinking it's medicine. Or maybe the monetary incentive of pharmacies could be
taken away: Homeopathy can only be sold with a 1% margin (or similar).</p>
<p>We could also add warnings to the packages like we have it on cigarettes:</p>
<ul>
<li>This product is NOT tested according to the medical standards.</li>
<li>This product did not prove effectiveness.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>

## Long-Term problems

I guess some problems will always be an issue:

* **Healthcare**: Which services should be covered? How much should be paid to
  doctors? How is it financed?
* **Employment**: How should we deal with unemployed people? How much do you
  have to pay for the insurance?
* **Poverty**: Some people have significantly less than others. How much
  redistribution of wealth is fair? Who gets something? How do people get
  money? Do they have to do something to get it?
