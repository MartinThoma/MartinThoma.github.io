### Breakthroughs in Energy production

Energy production became an even hotter topic in Germany when the Russo-Ukrainian war started. Germany depends a lot on Russian gas for heating. For Germany, there are only two options to go to at the moment: Solar and wind energy. We don’t want coal, gas, and oil due to climate change. We cannot expand energy generation by water. Uranium would also have to be imported from Russia and the waste problem is not solved. Geothermics seems not to be an option in most of Germany (although heat pumps are for sure a good idea!).

Interesting is also the [Levelized cost of energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levelized_cost_of_electricity) (LCOE). That is the total cost of producing one MWh of energy, including building the generators and dealing with the trash. The number depends heavily on the year and on the area as wages differ.

Where we were (February 2022, [source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ise/de/documents/publications/studies/aktuelle-fakten-zur-photovoltaik-in-deutschland.pdf)) with energy production 2021 ([source](https://www.bdew.de/presse/presseinformationen/erneuerbare-energien-deckten-im-jahr-2021-rund-42-prozent-des-stromverbrauchs/), [source](https://www.wind-energie.de/themen/zahlen-und-fakten/deutschland/), [source](https://www.iwr.de/news/deutscher-windmarkt-2021-weiter-am-boden-photovoltaik-zubau-stagniert-news37745), [source](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/163993/umfrage/beschaeftigte-in-der-deutschen-photovoltaikbranche), [source](https://www.e3dc.com/89-prozent-des-solarpotenzials-auf-deutschen-ein-und-zweifamilienhaeusern-sind-noch-ungenutzt/), [source](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/1169374/umfrage/anzahl-der-beschaeftigten-biomasse-in-deutschland/), [source](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/themen/klima-energie/erneuerbare-energien/erneuerbare-energien-in-zahlen#strom), [source](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/167671/umfrage/anzahl-der-biogasanlagen-in-deutschland-seit-1992/), [source](https://strom-report.de/photovoltaik/)). Keep in mind that 1 TWh = 1 billion kWh.

* **Wind**: produced (**land-based 92 TWh and 25 TWh water-based**); installed 56.13 GW; 121,700 jobs in Germany in 2018; 23,230 wind turbines. The highest one has 246m and is located in Stuttgart, Germany ([source](https://w3.windmesse.de/windenergie/news/26513-max-bogl-rekord-wasserbatterie)). In 2019, the LCOE was estimated at 37 USD/MWh for land-based wind turbines in the US ([source](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy21osti/78471.pdf)). Offshore turbines which are fixed on the ground of the sea were estimated at **85 USD/MWh** and floating offshore wind turbines at 132 USD/MWh. In 2021 for Germany the LCOE of land-based wind turbines was estimated at around **60 EUR/MWh** ([source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/en/press-media/press-releases/2021/levelized-cost-of-electricity-renewables-clearly-superior-to-conventional-power-plants-due-to-rising-co2-prices.html)).
* **Photovoltaics**: **produced 51 TWh**; installed 60 GW in 2021; 32,000 jobs in Germany in 2016; 2 million modules installed in 2021. Some are on buildings, some are on unused land, and in “agrivoltaic” one tries to combine photovoltaics with land used for agriculture.  The record lab cell efficiency is 26.7% for mono-crystalline and 24.4% for multi-crystalline silicon wafer-based technology. The highest lab efficiency in thin film technology is 23.4% for CIGS and 21.0% for CdTe solar cells. Record lab cell efficiency for Perovskite is 25.5%. The efficiency of average commercial wafer-based silicon modules is about 20%, and CdTe module efficiency is about 19%. In the laboratory, the best-performing modules are based on mono-crystalline silicon with 24.4% efficiency. In the laboratory, high-concentration multi-junction solar cells achieve an efficiency of up to 47.1% today. With concentrator technology, module efficiencies of up to 38.9% have been reached ([source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ise/de/documents/publications/studies/Photovoltaics-Report.pdf)). It’s important to realize that you don’t buy only solar cells, but also an inverter. The efficiency of the overall photovoltaic system is what you’re interested in; not only the cell efficiency. Typical inverters are string inverters, central inverters, microinverters, and power optimizers ([source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ise/de/documents/publications/studies/Photovoltaics-Report.pdf)). In 2021 in Germany, the LCOE of utility-scale photovoltaics was estimated at around **40 EUR/MWh** ([source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/en/press-media/press-releases/2021/levelized-cost-of-electricity-renewables-clearly-superior-to-conventional-power-plants-due-to-rising-co2-prices.html)).
* **Biomass**: produced **50 TWh**; installed 10.4 GW; 112,100 jobs in Germany in 2018; 9700 engines installed in 2020. [BioEnergie Park Güstrow](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/BioEnergie_Park_G%C3%BCstrow) is the biggest one and can contain 50,000 tons. In 2021 in Germany, the LCOE of biomass/biogas was estimated at around **120 EUR/MWh** ([source](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/en/press-media/press-releases/2021/levelized-cost-of-electricity-renewables-clearly-superior-to-conventional-power-plants-due-to-rising-co2-prices.html)).
* **Fusion**: General Fusion (Canada) works on magnetized target fusion — the devices look like Diesel engines. Helion Energy wants to use direct energy conversion using magnetic fields to simplify the system. Helion Energy wants to get in 2024 to the market.

What happened in 2022:

* Perovskite solar cells (PSC) had some breakthroughs. They are cheaper than silicon solar cells. People expect photovoltaic systems to get over 40% efficiency by combining the two in a tandem solar cell ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kaBkaHlWsk)). PSCs are vulnerable to humidity, UV light, and heat.

### Breakthroughs in Battery Technology

Battery Technology breakthroughs have a huge impact on our society. On the small scale, it improves our lives as we don’t have to worry so much about our smartphones — in 2021, they lasted about one day. Maximum two, if you actually used them. On a bit larger scale, the shortcomings of batteries are the main point that blocks the widespread adoption of electric cars. And on the scale of humanity, better battery technology will empower us to completely switch to renewable energy (photovoltaic and wind power).

Currently, we are at those stats with Lithium-Ion batteries:

* Gravimetric energy density (weight): 100–265 Wh/kg
* Volumetric energy density (volume): 250–693 Wh/L
* Cycle life: 400–1,200 cycles
* Charging temperature: 5 to 45 °C
* Consumer price: US\$132 / kWh (Li-Ion phosphate: \$80 to \$100/kWh)

[**The 2022 State of Battery Technology** *Batteries are extremely important. They are built into millions of devices, especially smartphones, cars, and homes…*](../the-2022-state-of-battery-technology/)

Let’s see what happened in 2022:

* 2022/04: PolyJoule put a plastic battery on the market ([source](https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/plastic-battery-grid-storage/)). They are already produced on a big scale ([source](https://www.elektronikpraxis.vogel.de/kunststoffakku-ist-robust-sicher-und-umweltfreundlich-a-1112463/)). They don’t need precious metals. They have a cycle life of 12,000 cycles and they can be operated from -40°C to +50°C ([source](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2022/04/14/us-start-up-develops-polymer-based-batteries-for-stationary-storage/)). Their consumer price is around US\$65 / kWh ([source](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/04/13/1049728/plastic-batteries-grid-storage/)). Their only drawback: Their volumetric energy density is about 5 times lower than the ones of Lithium-Ion batteries.
* 2022/04: Li-S crystal battery from Theion (a solid-state sulfur battery) claims a 1000-cycle life, no risk of catching fire, and a lot cheaper production and resource costs for the battery. They claim to achieve an energy density of 1,000 Wh/kg and 1,500 Wh/l with an operating temperature of -20 °C to 60 °C ([source](https://www.greencarcongress.com/2022/03/20220330-theion.html)). This year those batteries should go into space, in cars in 2024, and in phones from 2025. This sounds extremely good. So good that I’m getting skeptical.
* 2022/02: Li-S (Lithium-sulfur battery) had several breakthrough news. [One source](https://reneweconomy.com.au/australian-research-breakthrough-promises-greener-cheaper-faster-batteries/) claims that the cycle life could be at 2000 cycles, and [another group from Drexel University claims](https://reneweconomy.com.au/researchers-claim-major-breakthrough-in-li-s-batteries-that-could-revolutionise-batteries/) a cycle life of 4000 with a capacity that is “more than three-fold that of a lithium-ion battery”.
* 2021/01: Sodium-ion batteries might help to reduce reliance on rare minerals and be cheaper overall. It might also have an order of magnitude more lifecycles than Li-ion batteries ([source](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/ril-subsidiary-acquires-uk-based-sodium-ion-battery-tech-firm-faradion-121123100210_1.html), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvVdl2Ny1yM)). According to CATL, their batteries will cost \$40/kWh.
* 2021/11: [Blade Battery](https://evreporter.com/byd-blade-battery-what-makes-it-ultra-safe/) (an [LFP battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery))— 165 Wh/kg and 450 Wh/L; Cycle life of 3000 cycles! But the main point of this battery was operational safety: Byd claims that the battery is resistant to piercing damage and to very high temperatures.
* 2021/11: Nio announced a car with a solid-state battery for 2022 ([source](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/nio-s-150-kwh-solid-state-battery-pack-will-actually-be-a-semi-ssb-pack-175124.html)). VW had similar news ([source](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/tech-zukunft/alternative-antriebe/feststoffbatterien-vw-elektroauto-doppelte-reichweite-schnellladung-produktion-salzgitter/)).
* 2021/09: TU Dresden — a German University — built a Flywheel with 500 kWh capacity with a weight of 20,000 kg ([source 1](https://www.mdr.de/wissen/faszination-technik/tu-dresden-baut-neuartigen-energiespeicher-100.html), [source 2](https://www.en-former.com/tu-dresden-baut-riesigen-schwungradspeicher-fuer-windkraftanlagen/)). Flywheels lose between 3%-20% per hour. For this reason, they are only good as short-term storage.
* 2021/08: GAC claims that the Aion V (6C version) electric car should be able to fully charge in 8 minutes ([source](https://www.derstandard.de/story/2000129125297/durchbruch-chinesischer-e-suv-in-acht-minuten-vollgeladen), [source](https://efahrer.chip.de/news/nur-eine-zigarettenlaenge-zum-laden-china-stromer-laesst-konkurrenz-alt-aussehen_105652)). This should be possible by a new graphene battery.
* 2021/06: Dual Fluid reactor received almost 7 Million Canadian Dollars ([source](https://www.modernpowersystems.com/news/newsnuclear-startup-raises-millions-in-seed-funding-8839680))
* 2021/05: Graphene Aluminum-Ion battery (G+Al Battery) claims to charge 60 times faster, cost only \$9/kWh, and have a gravimetric energy density of 300 Wh/kg ([source](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaeltaylor/2021/05/13/ev-range-breakthrough-as-new-aluminum-ion-battery-charges-60-times-faster-than-lithium-ion/?sh=5749d8576d28), [source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5B6icvUBNzE))
* 2020/12: Fraunhofer Institute develops SALD technology ([source](https://www.finanzen.net/nachricht/geld-karriere-lifestyle/sald-technologie-1-000-kilometer-reichweite-kein-problem-fraunhofer-institut-entwickelt-ultraleistungsfaehigen-akku-9536325))
* 2019/06: ETES, a high-temperature energy storage, was built in Hamburg, Germany ([source](https://www.energieforschung.de/aktuelles/news/archiv?backRef=15&news=Windenergie_in_Vulkangestein_speichern_ndash_Weltpremiere_in_Hamburg)). It can store up to 130 MWh for up to a week by heating 1,000,000 kg of volcanic rock. The downside of it is that the efficiency is only at 22% ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7-afimbza6I)).
