Current times are hard. Energy prices are high in Germany due to the supply cut from Russian gas, the supply interruptions of Ukrainian grain cause food prices to rise, and supply chain interruptions caused by Corona from Chinese goods are troublesome. The interest rates are suddenly way higher than they have been in several years which increases house building costs and makes buying a home harder. Inflation is rising due to sudden scarcity of common goods. While nobody I know has to fear to live in a cold apartment in winter or not have food on the table, many people cannot afford as much as before. The wealth of the ordinary Joe was painfully and suddenly reduced.

I see many doomsday-videos on YouTube how things will become a lot worse. I see Tweets pointing out hardship.

While it’s true that we are going through a difficult time, I believe that things will get better. Here’s why:

## №1: A shift towards Energy independence

Germany was dependent on Russian gas for a long time. Way longer than necessary. While the advantages of renewable energy for the climate are obvious for a long time, they only became the economical energy source of choice in the past 10 years or so. Wind and solar are for sure the two big ones, with a little bit of water and a tiny amount of different energy sources such as geothermal and tidal energy.

The key point on a private level now is that the economic advantage became very obvious while the technology is mature enough to no longer be an experiment. Solar panels on your house became a conservative investment.

The key point on a government level is autonomy. It became obvious that energy independence is directly connected with political power. Instead of trading dependence on Russian gas with American LNG or other countries’ fossil fuels, we will work towards independence. Maybe not in the next ten years, but in the long run. I can also imagine that the rest of the world will keep this situation in mind.

## №2: Climate Protection and Environmental Protection

Becoming energy independent will also let us achieve two other goals: Protecting the climate by reducing greenhouse gas emissions and protecting our environment by reducing particulate matter (PM). PM is the very fine dust in the exhaust of cars.

If we want to become energy independent, we need to replace oil and gas. That means we need to replace the heaters in our homes by heat pumps. It also means that most private cars need to be electric. Buses and trucks might switch to hydrogen.

## №3: Open Culture

People like to share. Not (only) in the social media sense, but in the sense that creators like to make parts of their content publicly available for free. You can read [/r/nosleep](https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/) for horror stories, [/r/writingPrompts](https://www.reddit.com/r/WritingPrompts/) for a mix of short stories, [/r/cryosleep](https://www.reddit.com/r/cryosleep/) and [/r/HFY](https://www.reddit.com/r/HFY/) for SciFi. You can watch thousands of YouTube videos. You can read a lot of blog articles. New tools like [ChatGPT](../7-ways-chatgpt-falls-short-in-question-answering/) and [NightCafe](https://creator.nightcafe.studio/) (Text2Image, DALL-E) make it easier than ever to create content.

**Free music** is for sure also a thing, isn’t it? I’ve seen amazing clips by [Paint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=diU70KshcjA), [Zoe Anne](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EVizoJCIz4), and [Peter Hollens](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-puiI2d_yL4).

And there are rules on how long the creative content can be protected. I think it’s something like 70 years after the death of the creator? That means our grandchildren will hopefully profit from the explosion of creativity we had in the past 20 years.

## №4: Open Tech

Programmers share their knowledge and create **free software**. The most prominent example is Linux, but there are thousands of other examples. The value created like this is hard to guess — in 2018 in the EU only it is estimated to have created €65 billion ([source](https://github.blog/2022-01-20-open-source-creates-value-but-how-do-you-measure-it/)).

Although Open Source is by far the biggest one, there are also **Open Hardware** projects. The [Global Village Construction Set](https://www.opensourceecology.org/gvcs/) is one of the awesome projects in this area.

The bulk of open hardware projects likely is around **3D printing**. I assume especially cheap replacement parts for bigger machines that were not 3D printed create the biggest value.

## №5: Advancements in 2022

We focus on the bad news. It’s more important to fix things that go wrong, but once in a while it’s worth pointing out all of the small gradual improvements:

* **Medicine**: Advancements in [treating Alzheimer’s disease](https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2022/12/08/1141396375/lecanemab-alzheimer-drug-researchers-hope), [HIV](https://eu.usatoday.com/story/news/health/2022/12/30/hiv-injection-treatment-lenacapavir-approved-fda/10959568002/), [cancer](https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/study-of-potentially-cancer-stopping-pill-recruiting-volunteers/), [RSV](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/us-fda-grants-priority-review-pfizers-rsv-vaccine-2022-12-07/), a [Malaria vaccine](https://www.dailysabah.com/life/health/biontech-begins-early-human-trials-of-mrna-based-malaria-vaccine). mRNA vaccines in general — there is a lot more to come!
* **Social Advancements**: Working from home became more accepted ([example](https://www.wired.com/story/remote-work-labor-economy/)), a [female crash test dummy](https://www.weforum.org/agenda/2022/12/female-crash-test-dummies-road-safety-gender-equality/) was developed, [reduced alcohol consumption](https://www.washingtonpost.com/magazine/2022/07/25/cocktails-sober-curious-drinking-alcohol/), reduced meat consumption ([example](https://www.veganfoodandliving.com/news/ikea-plant-based-restaurant-dishes/))
* **Technology**: [Battery tech](../the-2022-state-of-battery-technology/), Generative AI in the form of [ChatGPT](../chatgpt-the-new-ai-tool-everybody-is-talking-about/) (Chatbot) and DALL·E 2 / Stable Diffusion for text-to-image (see [Nightcafe](https://creator.nightcafe.studio/) if you want to give it a shot). [AlphaFold](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-022-04438-1) for protein folding is also remarkable. [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum) moved from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake. A breakthrough in [nuclear fusion](https://www.bbc.com/news/science-environment-63950962) was reached.
