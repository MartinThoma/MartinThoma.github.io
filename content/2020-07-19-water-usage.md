I like comparisons. Recently, for an [article about Homeopathy](../homoeopathie/), I compared a drop of water with big lakes, oceans and finally the sun.
But I lack references of water usage in my everyday life. Let's change that.

Some comparisons:

* A day of drinking is a quarter of one toilet flush.
* A 10-minute shower with a big shower head is about as much as a full bathtub.
  The shower head matters more than the decision between shower and bath.
* The average German uses about 80 times as much water per day as they drink.
* The water we use directly is only a small part of our footprint: food,
  clothes and other products need far more water in their production.
