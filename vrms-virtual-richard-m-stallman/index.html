I've just installed vrms - the virtual Richard Stallman :D

Richard Stallman is an American software freedom activist and computer programmer. He launched the GNU Project to create a free Unix-like operating system, and he has been the project's lead architect and organizer. With the launch of the GNU Project, he initiated the free software movement; he founded the Free Software Foundation.
