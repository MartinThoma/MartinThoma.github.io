Yes. This blog post is about washing clothes. Nothing fancy, but something most people need to look up once in a while.

The following symbols are defined in EN ISO 3758:2005 "Textiles -- Care labelling code using symbols".

Waschmittel

The following is in German. I was too lazy to look the words up.
  • Vollwaschmittel: Hat Bleichmittel
  • Feinwaschmittel: Keine Aufheller oder Bleichmittel; für empfindliche Wäsche

Die Verwendung spezieller Feinwaschmittel für Schwarzes bzw. dunkle Kleidung brachte bei einem Test der Stiftung Warentest keinen Vorteil gegenüber einem herkömmlichen Fein- oder Buntwaschmittel.

Source: Wikipedia / Stiftung Warentest

Turning inside out

For trousers, T-shirts and pullovers, I recommend turning them inside-out while washing. This way, the side that people see is protected from getting scratches.

For anything with zippers, I recommend closing them. This way, the zipper itself cannot be damaged and other clothes cannot be damaged by the zipper.
