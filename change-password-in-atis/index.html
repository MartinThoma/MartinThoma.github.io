Change password in ATIS

Jul 12, 2014 · by Martin Thoma

Recently, the Heartbleed bug was discovered. It works like this:

One effect of that bug is that you have to change your password.

You can do that from home via SSH. Just replace s_thoma by s_[your last name]:

$ ssh s_thoma@i08fs1.ira.uka.de
s_thoma@i08fs1.ira.uka.de's password:
s_thoma@i08fs1(~)$ passwd
Changing password for user s_thoma.
Enter login(AD) password:
Current Password:
New password:
Retype new password:
AD password information changed for s_thoma
passwd: all authentication tokens updated successfully.
s_thoma@i08fs1(~)$ exit
logout
Connection to i08fs1.ira.uka.de closed.

In case you don't remember your password: Take a look at your Browser settings. You've probably entered it already for looking at your printing account and eventually your browser saved it.
