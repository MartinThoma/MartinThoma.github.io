Cantuccini

Dez 24, 2019 · by Martin Thoma

  • Cooking 21
  • Italian 2
  • Recipe 17
Contents
  • Ingredients
  • Tools
  • Preparation

Cantucci are Italian almond biscuits. It takes roughly 1 hour to prepare them plus 1 hour to let them rest.

Ingredients

  • 520 g flour
  • 360 g sugar
  • 180 g Almond (peeled / without the skin)
  • 180 g Hazelnut (peeled / without the shell)
  • 80 g Marzipan
  • 50 g butter (soft)
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 16 g vanilla sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Lebkuchengewürz
  • salt

Tools

  • Hand mixer
  • Sheet pan and oven
  • Plastic wrap
  • Fridge
  • Knife

Preparation

  1. Blend everything (except the Hazelnut and Almond) to a fine dough.
  2. Make sure the almonds and hazelnuts don't have any skin and knead them into the dough (German: "unterkneten").
  3. Divide the dough into 4 pieces of equal size.
  4. Roll each piece into a 35 cm long roll.
  5. Put the rolls in plastic wrap and in a fridge.
  6. Wait for 1h.
  7. Heat the oven to 200 °C (convection: 180 °C).
  8. Put the rolls on a sheet pan and in the oven for 15 minutes.
  9. Get the rolls out of the oven, cut them into 1cm thick slices and let them cool for 15 minutes.
  10. Turn the cantuccini so that they lie on the cut surface on the sheet pan.
  11. Put them in the oven again for 8-10 minutes until they have a nice brown color.
