Insoles

Jan 25, 2019 · by Martin Thoma

Contents

  • Other

Shoes come in many shapes, but they don't fit perfectly for every foot. While we choose shoes by length, feet also differ in width, height, and the area which touches the floor when standing on a flat surface. Insoles can make a shoe which has the right length / enough width more comfortable.

My "Außenrist" (top part of my foot) usually hurts after longer hikes. I want to fix that. The idea is that I can maybe push some parts of my foot more up, so that the force is more equally distributed.

I tried the currex EdgePro one for a week or so, but my calves began to hurt. Then I switched to the Meindl Vacuum MFS. Way better. Also way cheaper (13 EUR vs 44 EUR).

Currex

SuperFeet

Profile heights (arch support) are measured without weight by Frugal Foot:

  • Green: Original, most popular, for high arches
  • merinoGrey: Green one covered by merino wool
  • Orange: 3cm profile height
  • Blue: 2.5cm profile height, for medium arches
  • Berry: 2.8cm profile height, women-specific (slimmer heel and arch)
  • Black: 2.4cm profile height, slimmest, lowest profile, for low arches
  • Carbon: 2.8cm profile height
  • hotPink: 2.0cm profile height, women-specific (narrower), foil lining at forefoot to make it warmer
  • Yellow: 2.5cm profile height, for skating / ice skates
  • BlackDMP: 3.3cm profile height, Dynamic Molding Process (Memory Foam); for people with sensitive feet
  • 3/4: For work; they are not full length to make it easier to fit inside
