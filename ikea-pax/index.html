IKEA PAX

Apr 30, 2018 · by Martin Thoma

  • House 39
  • Review 33
Contents
  • What I like
  • What I miss
  • Assembling

IKEA's PAX system is a modular wardrobe system. I bought:

  • 901.839.91: PAX Korpus Kleiderschrank: 236cm x 50cm x 58cm (H x W x D)
  • 902.568.93: Komplement Kleiderstange
  • 502.463.06: Komplement Schublade
  • 702.779.81: Komplement Boden

It was nice to combine with MALM:

  • 203.598.23: Schreibtisch, Eichenfurnier weiß lasiert
  • 091.750.57: Bettgestell hoch
  • 702.787.06: Lönset Federholzrahmen

What I like

  • All manuals are online as PDF. You just need to remember the article numbers.
  • You can combine things to what you need.

What I miss

  • If you choose to have doors, there are 18cm until the first drawer can begin. There don't seem to be any IKEA boxes of the necessary size.
  • You can choose to put the front at the bottom of the frame (Korpus) either directly at the front or about 1cm further inside. I now have two frames where I chose one depth on one side and the other depth on the other side. Sometimes less choice is better.

Assembling

I missed one hint when assembling the cabinet: Use many nails for the back of it! When you use too few, things can fall behind.
