Unreal Tournament 2004

Dez 31, 2012 · by Martin Thoma

  • Games 15

I am currently at home and I found Unreal Tournament 2004 in my old stuff. Of course, I had to play it again ☺

Before you start, you might want to get the latest version. You have to find the patches by yourself. According to this page, you get all updates / bonus content with "Mega Pack" (201 MB).

It's astonishing that there is neither a possibility to upgrade the game within the game, nor an official source for patches. I guess providing patches is an easy way to spread viruses. Do I have any chance to check if it's a valid patch or a patch with a virus?
