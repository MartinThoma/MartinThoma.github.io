Fleece

Dez 3, 2018 · by Martin Thoma

  • Gear 7
  • Hiking 10
  • Trekking 9

Another product article for Nepal 🙂 - this time it is about fleece sweaters. Fleece sweaters are a mid-layer. You have three layers:

  • Base Layer: On your skin
  • Mid Layer: Insulation
  • Outer Layer: Against wind / rain / snow

This means the fleece is not meant to be windproof or waterproof, but to keep you warm. I will use it for trekking. They don't insulate as well as down; I think I will use them on the hike. The down is more for when I don't move.

Polartec Thermal Pro-Fleece seems to be a common material; Nanuk is the version from Jack Wolfskin.
